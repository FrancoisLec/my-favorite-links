My Favorite Links

Version

Save, organize, tag and search your favorite web links — right from Chrome.

Quick help

  • Ctrl+Shift+Y or the ★ button: save the current page or search your links.
  • Right-click any page or link → Save to My Favorite Links.
  • In the manager, press / to search and drag a tile onto a folder to move it.

Privacy

Your links are stored in your Chrome profile and synced by Chrome to your own Google account, so they follow you to your other computers. Nothing is sent to any other server.
